# Sample assets and architectural material

The MIT license covers Charlie Holland's original application code, tooling and documentation. It does not transfer or grant rights in the supplied architectural design or in third-party product designs.

The repository includes a sample floor plan and a derived model so the viewer can demonstrate the same space as a blueprint, 3D overview and first-person walkthrough. The following are sample project material excluded from the MIT grant:

- `src/assets/source-plan.svg` and `src/assets/model.json`;
- plan-derived architectural layout, coordinates and dimensions represented within the application;
- screenshots, GIFs and other depictions of that sample architectural design;
- those same sample assets when embedded in `dist/unit-101-walkthrough.html`.

Their inclusion for demonstration does not grant permission to reuse the underlying architectural design. The original DWG is not included. Source-identifying diagnostic metadata and an empty external drawing-link block have been removed; the model's functional geometry is retained.

Third-party textures and Three.js retain their own CC0 and MIT terms respectively, as recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Appliance references do not grant rights in Samsung names, branding or product designs.

The supplied drawing is a 2D plan. The 3.0 m ceiling height, vertical details, finishes, furniture, lighting and appliance choices are illustrative. This example is a spatial study, not a construction model or a calibrated daylight analysis.
