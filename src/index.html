ABOUT THE STUDY

From a plan to a place.

An interactive architectural study by Charlie Holland. Explore the relationship between the floor plan, a 3D cutaway and an eye-level walkthrough.

What is modelled
The apartment layout, wall footprints, openings and furniture positions are derived from a supplied 2D drawing. Eight spaces can be explored, including the kitchen, bedroom and balcony.
What is illustrative
The 3.0 m ceiling, finishes, lighting, furniture heights and joinery details are provisional. This is a spatial study, not a construction model or a calibrated daylight analysis.
Explore your way
In 3D overview, drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. In Walk through, use W/A/S/D or the arrow keys to move, drag to look, and Q/E to turn. On touch screens, use the directional buttons. Select a room to jump there.
Take the guided tour
Play tour visits all eight spaces in about 96 seconds at the default 1.5× speed. Pause, restart or change speed at any time. Manual movement pauses the tour; Resume continues from your position.
Model limits
The drawing is a 2D plan, not a fully specified 3D building model.
